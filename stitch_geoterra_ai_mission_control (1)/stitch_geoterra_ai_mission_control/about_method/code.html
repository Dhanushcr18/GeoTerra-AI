Ethics, Bias & Responsible AI Principles

Protective framework governing inference limits and civil authority triage.

gavel

Non-Accusatory Alert Framing Policy

Directive #ETH-01

Satellite pixel variation can stem from seasonal agriculture, authorized civil earthworks, or micro-droughts. GeoTerra AI alerts strictly avoid criminalizing or definitive classifications. All notices are labeled:

"Potential suspicious activity: requires official ground verification before enforcement."
visibility_off

Strict Area-Level Aggregation & Zero Individual Tracking

Directive #ETH-02

Algorithms reject any sub-tile targeting beneath 1.5 hectares for civilian land alterations. The system cannot resolve faces, license plates, residential identity footprints, or individual movements. Designed exclusively for aggregate infrastructure protection and relief logistics.

verified

Dual-Sensor Cross Validation & Hallucination Checks

Directive #ETH-03

Specular reflection from urban glass roofs or asphalt runways often mimics standing water on single-pass SAR. GeoTerra requires cross-checking against optical NDWI baselines or DEM slope bounds (>8° slope water runoff rejection) prior to queuing emergency dispatch vectors.
