Limitations & Failure Modes

flight_takeoff
Specular Runway False-Positives Extremely smooth dry asphalt (e.g., airport runways, wide highways) causes forward radar scattering similar to standing water bodies. Mitigated by an OSM infrastructure exclusion layer.
forest
Dense Canopy Penetration Limits C-band SAR (5.6 cm wavelength) volume-scatters inside thick evergreen canopy, hiding understory floods. Requires optical NDWI synthesis or longer L-band radar.
rocket_launch
Upcoming: NISAR L-Band Integration Dual-frequency NISAR (NASA-ISRO SAR) pipeline planned for Q3 2025 will deploy 24 cm L-band telemetry to directly penetrate high-density forest canopies in the Brahmaputra Basin.
